Does Enhancing Reels to 4K Actually Get More Views?

Yes — and here is exactly how it works. Instagram and YouTube both use video quality as a ranking signal when deciding which content to show to wider audiences. When you upload a 1080p video, the platform compresses it further during processing, which degrades quality. When you upload a 4K video, two things happen:

  • Less compression damage: The platform compresses from 4K down to display resolution, preserving more detail than compressing from 1080p.
  • Quality signal boost: The algorithm reads the high-resolution metadata and tags your video as high-quality content, making it more likely to be recommended.

This is why many top creators and digital marketers upscale their videos to 4K before uploading — even when the original footage is 1080p. Our tool does this using Lanczos filtering, the same upscaling method used in professional video editing software.

How to Extract MP3 from MP4 Video Online Free

Extracting audio from a video is useful for many situations — saving a song from a downloaded video, extracting voiceover for a podcast, getting background music from a tutorial, or converting a video lecture into an audio file for offline listening.

Switch to the Extract Audio tab, upload your video, choose MP3 (for sharing) or WAV (for studio editing), and click Extract Audio. The tool uses FFmpeg's LAME encoder to produce 320kbps MP3 — the highest standard MP3 quality — or PCM WAV for full lossless audio.
