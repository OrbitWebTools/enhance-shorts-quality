The Creator's Guide: Why do my Reels look blurry?

Every creator has been there: Your video looks incredibly sharp in your camera roll, but the moment you upload it to Instagram or YouTube Shorts, it looks pixelated, blurry, and compressed. Why?

The Algorithm's Compression: Social media platforms process millions of videos daily. To save server space, they aggressively compress your 1080p videos. When you upload a standard video, their system crushes the bitrate, destroying your visual quality.

The 4K Hack to Beat the Algorithm

Top creators and editors use a simple workaround: Upscaling to 4K before uploading. By feeding Instagram a 4K file, the platform's compression engine has much more data to work with. Even when they compress it, the resulting video stays significantly sharper, retaining crisp edges and vibrant colors.
